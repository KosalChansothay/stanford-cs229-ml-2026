CS229: មូលដ្ឋានគ្រឹះ និងទម្រង់នៃការរៀនរបស់ម៉ាស៊ីន

ទិដ្ឋភាពទូទៅនៃវគ្គសិក្សា

CS229 គឺជាវគ្គសិក្សាមូលដ្ឋានគ្រឹះនៃការរៀនរបស់ម៉ាស៊ីន (Machine Learning) នៅសាកលវិទ្យាល័យ Stanford ដែលគ្របដណ្តប់លើ ការរៀនដោយមានការណែនាំ (Supervised Learning), ការរៀនដោយគ្មានការណែនាំ (Unsupervised Learning), ការរៀនបែបពង្រឹង (Reinforcement Learning), និងទម្រង់នៃការរៀនស៊ីជម្រៅទំនើបៗ (Modern Deep Learning Paradigms)។ វគ្គសិក្សានេះសង្កត់ធ្ងន់ជាចម្បងលើការបង្កើតគំរូគណិតវិទ្យា ការទាញរូបមន្ត និងមូលដ្ឋានទ្រឹស្តីក្បួនដោះស្រាយ។

បញ្ជីកំណត់ចំណាំមេរៀន

  • មេរៀនទី ១: សេចក្តីផ្តើម (Introduction)

    ទិដ្ឋភាពទូទៅនៃវគ្គសិក្សា, ទម្រង់ ML, និយមន័យរបស់ Mitchell ភាសាខ្មែរ

    សេចក្តីផ្តើមអំពីទម្រង់ និងទស្សនវិជ្ជានៃ CS229, គោលការណ៍ដាក់ពិន្ទុ, និងសសរស្តម្ភទាំងបីនៃការរៀនរបស់ម៉ាស៊ីន៖ ការរៀនដោយមានការណែនាំ ការរៀនដោយគ្មានការណែនាំ និងការរៀនបែបពង្រឹង។

  • មេរៀនទី ២: Supervised Learning Setup

    Linear regression, ERM, gradient descent, normal equations

    Formal setup of supervised learning with linear regression, least squares cost function, and optimization via batch gradient descent and normal equations.

  • មេរៀនទី ៣: Logistic Regression

    MLE, sigmoid link function, gradient ascent, Newton's method

    Probabilistic justification of least squares via maximum likelihood, logistic regression for binary classification, and the SGD vs. Newton's method trade-off.

  • មេរៀនទី ៤: Exponential Family, GLMs & Classification

    Exponential family, generalized linear models, softmax regression, label smoothing

    A unifying framework for distributions via the exponential family, the three assumptions of GLMs, softmax regression for multi-class classification, and label smoothing.

  • មេរៀនទី ៥: Gaussian Discriminant Analysis

    Generative learning, GDA, Naive Bayes, Laplace smoothing

    The shift from discriminative to generative modeling: Gaussian Discriminant Analysis, its connection to logistic regression, Naive Bayes for text classification, and Laplace smoothing.

  • មេរៀនទី ៦: Dataset Split, ML Advice

    Bias-variance, regularization, double descent, cross-validation, hyperparameter tuning

    Diagnostic workflows for ML: bias-variance trade-off, L2 regularization, double descent phenomenon, hold-out vs. cross-validation, and multi-fidelity hyperparameter tuning via Hyperband.

  • មេរៀនទី ៧: Neural Networks Architecture

    Neural networks, nonlinear activations, universal approximation, forward pass

    From generalized linear models to multi-layer perceptrons: activations, expressivity, and GPU-efficient forward-pass vectorization.

  • មេរៀនទី ៨: Backpropagation & Training

    Backpropagation, chain rule, computational graphs, Adam, autodiff

    Matrix calculus and computational graphs: reverse-mode autodiff, chain rule derivations, numerical stability, and adaptive gradient optimizers.

  • មេរៀនទី ៩: K-Means & Gaussian Mixture Models

    Unsupervised clustering, K-Means, soft assignments, Gaussian Mixture Models

    Unsupervised grouping and density estimation: coordinate descent in K-Means, soft assignments, and the Gaussian Mixture Model formulation.

  • មេរៀនទី ១០: Expectation-Maximization & PCA

    EM algorithm, Jensen's inequality, ELBO, Principal Component Analysis

    The general EM algorithm via Jensen's inequality and evidence lower bound (ELBO), followed by Principal Component Analysis via eigen-decomposition and SVD.

  • មេរៀនទី ១១: Generative Diffusion Models

    Diffusion models, forward noise, score matching, reverse sampling, guidance

    Modern generative models: forward noising process, score-based reverse sampling, denoising score matching, and classifier-free guidance.

  • មេរៀនទី ១២: Foundation Models & Fine-Tuning

    Foundation models, scaling, in-context learning, LoRA, quantization

    Pre-training paradigms, in-context learning, parameter-efficient fine-tuning via Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA), and model compression.

  • មេរៀនទី ១៣: Contrastive Learning & RAG

    Contrastive learning, InfoNCE, CLIP, vector search, RAG pipelines

    Self-supervised representation learning via InfoNCE loss, multi-modal alignment, vector databases, and retrieval-augmented generation pipelines.

  • មេរៀនទី ១៤: Transformers & Self-Attention

    Transformers, scaled dot-product attention, multi-head attention, FlashAttention

    The Transformer architecture from first principles: scaled dot-product attention, multi-head projections, positional encodings, and IO complexity.
